## conjectures.io figures, 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13

| Paid to solvers | $85,328 | $87,708 |
| Reward pool (how-it-works page) | $42,355 | $44,135 |

- The site's dollar figures are estimates at the current rate of its alpha token. The paid total
  rose about 2.8% and the pool about 4.2% in a day with no new reward paid, so most of the change is
  the exchange rate, not new money. For example, the 11 November payout for Erdős problem 12 part iii
  was shown as $4,530 yesterday and $4,656 today.
- "Lean proofs accepted" went from 54 to 55 while "Rewards paid" stayed at 40. The homepage does not
  say which problem the new accepted proof is for. A reward is paid only after a human reviewer also
  approves it, so an accepted proof does not always mean a payout.
- The nine problems featured on the homepage each show a bounty of $3,491.
- The homepage now says "Tensor kernels" is live (19 kernels across six model families, 7 boards).
  It replaces the ended "TorchLean kernels" competition. This is a separate, kernel-speed track, not the
  Lean bounty catalogue.

## SAIR Foundation: still no Stage 2 rules

- The SAIR GitHub organisation still has 8 repositories. lean-kernel-challenge was last updated on
  2026-09-17, and there is still no Stage 2 repository for the Lean Kernel Challenge or the Inverse
  Galois Challenge.
- The Lean Kernel Challenge rules still say Stage 1 closes "November 20, 2026, 23:59 AoE (UTC−12)".
  The final evaluation "runs separately after the deadline", with no date given, and Stage 2 "begins:
  December 2026". For each team and problem, the latest formal entry counts, not the best one.
