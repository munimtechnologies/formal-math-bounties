## The 16 November pool change

The site's "What changed" page (conjectures.io/problems/changes) lists for 16 Nov 2026:

- **2 solved:** "Erdős problem 94 - regular ngon" and "Erdős problem 1068". The page does not say
  who solved them or whether a reward is attached; the paid count is unchanged at 40, while Lean
  accepted rose by one and two submissions await reward.
- **4 added:** "Borger De Smit Question 4", "Hilbert 12 Lambda Geometric", "Hilbert 16 Quintic
  Surfaces" and "Hilbert 6 Vlasov Poisson Limit".

The validator repository explains the new Hilbert statements in three review folders
(github.com/conjectures-io/conjectures-validator, docs/review-decisions/):

- `2026-11-16-hilbert6-12-open-statements/` adds:
  - **Hilbert 12, `Hilbert12LambdaGeometric`**: every number field's maximal abelian extension is
    Λ-geometric. The README calls this the platform's universal reading of a question of Borger and
    de Smit, which the paper itself does not pose.
  - **Hilbert 12, `BorgerDeSmitQuestion4`**: Question 4 of Borger and de Smit (arXiv:1809.02295,
    which the README describes as an unrefereed preprint), taken verbatim. The README says the two
    Hilbert 12 statements are coupled: "In every consistent outcome exactly two of the four proof
    and refutation rewards are payable."
  - **Hilbert 6, `VlasovPoissonMeanFieldLimit`**: the mean-field limit from Newtonian point charges
    to the 3D Vlasov-Poisson equation, in two parts, an existence part (a known theorem that still
    has to be formalized) and the convergence part, which is the open one.
  - It replaces a 15 November finding that Hilbert 6 and general Hilbert 12 had no reward target.
  - Its own limit, quoted: all reviews and literature searches used the same model family as the
    author, with no human or cross-model review.
- `2026-11-16-hilbert12-combined/` publishes `Hilbert12.Hilbert12Combined` (status: "The
  proposition remains open") and warns that any proposed construction needs mathematical review of
  how it defines the special points.
- `2026-11-15-hilbert16-open-cases/` admits **Hilbert 16, quintic surfaces**: a qualifying real
  quintic surface has a real zero locus in RP³ with at most 23 connected components. The README
  says degree five is the first degree where the maximum is unknown ("It is 23, 24 or 25"; Orevkov's
  2001 construction gives 23). It holds back two other Hilbert 16 statements: the quadratic
  limit-cycle bound H(2) <= 4, "because a live unrefereed preprint claims exactly this result"
  (arXiv:2103.07193v4), and the general uniform bound, which a September manuscript claims. It
  also says: "No human mathematician reviewed this packet."
- The rank-one abelian Stark route for Hilbert 12, added on 13 November, "remains a separate active
  target".

Anyone thinking of attempting one of these should read the review folder first, including the
statement's audit and the site's rule on capped defect awards for faulty statements.
