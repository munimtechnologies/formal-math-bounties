## One deadline worth knowing now: 1 December 2026

| Programme | Who can apply | Money | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simons Foundation, **Targeted Grants to Math+AI Institutes (2027 RFA)** | Established U.S. and non-U.S. public or private educational and nonprofit **institutes and research centers** with a physical space | Up to **$500,000 over two years** per grant (20% indirect included), about $250,000 a year; up to **five** grants from Simons, with OpenAI and Anthropic possibly funding more through the same call | **2026-12-01, noon US Eastern**, through Simons Award Manager |

What it pays for: conferences, workshops, summer schools and scientific meetings (meals, travel,
lodging, facility fees), plus people inside the funded institute (visiting scholars, postdocs,
research associates, graduate students, administrative staff). Awards start between 2027-03-01 and
2027-06-01 and are not renewable.

Who it is **not** for: individuals, companies, consortia or initiatives without a physical space,
institutes that mainly serve one university's faculty, mainly experimental national labs, and
institutions in China. It does not pay PI summer salary or the salary of an institute's scientific
leadership, and it is not core funding for running or founding an institute.

This RFA is the open call behind the **$15 million** initiative that the Simons Foundation,
Anthropic and OpenAI announced on **2026-10-15** to help mathematicians check AI-assisted results,
learn new tools and set shared standards for AI use. OpenAI's own announcement confirms its
$5 million share; the release does not break down the other partners' shares. Anthropic and OpenAI
also offer AI tools and computing resources to some Simons-supported researchers.

## XTX Markets' 2026 update (dated 2026-09-16), for the record

- **Lean FRO:** a new **$10 million** for core operating costs through 2030 (after an earlier
  $10 million).
- **Mathlib Initiative:** a new **$10 million** for core operating costs through 2030 (after an
  earlier $6.5 million).
- **AIMO:** closed, and the Grand Prize will not be run. XTX gives the series totals as **$5,582,880
  advertised and about $1,619,760 awarded**, with 8,145 participants. Unspent money moves to other
  AI-for-maths work.
- **SAIR Foundation:** XTX funds three SAIR competitions: Andrews-Curtis Conjecture Challenge
  (Stage 1), Lean Kernel Challenge (Stage 1) and **Inverse Galois Problem Challenge (Stage 2)**.
  No amount is stated, and as of this check no Stage 2 rules or dates for the Inverse Galois
  challenge had been published.

What this means for an individual formalizer: none of these grants pays contributors per proof.
They pay for organisations' operations, meetings and staff. The lanes that pay individuals per
result are still the ones in the 2026-11-10 status (conjectures.io, paid in a crypto token, and the
Justin Sun Prize register, with no payments shown yet).
