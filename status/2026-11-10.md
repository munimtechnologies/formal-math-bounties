## Details

### Justin Sun Prize: legacy register and MathDB pilot
- Legacy register (awards/README.md): JSP-000239 and JSP-000327 (solutions, review ended
  2026-10-14), JSP-000526 (solution and Lean), JSP-000866 and JSP-001001 (solutions), and
  JSP-000305, -000371 and -000381 (Lean). The register states no amounts and has no payment
  column. In its own words, "Closing an issue, elapsed notice or an agent notification is not
  payment evidence."
- MathDB pilot (docs/award-process-mathdb.md, problems/mathdb-pilot.md): the 50 pilot problems
  show no Candidate or Awarded entry. **A clause that matters for expositions:** "Solution and
  Exposition awards are both gated by verification of a complete Lean formalization of the
  original problem." An exposition of a problem that is still open, however good, cannot be
  paid until that problem is solved and the solution formalized in Lean. Every recipient must
  pass identity verification. The register records no reward amounts and no currency.
- Correction to the README: the Expositions section calls MV2's five exposition repos "not
  submitted because the pilot is inactive." That is true, but the clause above is the bigger
  reason. All five (Legendre, Hadamard, Kakeya, Littlewood, Lehmer) are open problems, so
  none of them can earn the exposition share while the problems stay open.

### conjectures.io (Bittensor subnet; pays for kernel-checked Lean proofs)
- Rules (conjectures.io/how-it-works): "Use any model, agent or tool". The original AI chat
  logs that produced the proof are required, and a reviewer reads them. "Prove it or refute
  it: both count, and the first accepted answer takes the bounty". A proof of a faulty
  statement "can earn a capped defect award". Checking a file is free, and each submitted
  attempt costs one credit (0.25 τ), paid in TAO or other crypto. Bounties are paid in the
  subnet's alpha token to a Bittensor wallet. Dollar figures are the site's estimates. A new
  problem opens at 2.5% of the reward pool and rises to 12.5% over 15 days.
- Recent payouts on the homepage: $5,253 for Erdős problem 156 (6 Nov 2026); $870 and $2,002
  (28 Oct); $1,606 (14 Oct).
- The open bounties shown are mostly Erdős problems at $2,234-$5,187 each, and most are
  genuinely open research problems. Two of them sit under problems that erdosproblems.com
  marks "proved (Lean)": **Erdős 13 "general"** (the r-fold version, for every r, of the bound
  Bedert proved for r = 2) and **Erdős 49 "primes maximal"** (is f(N) = π(N) for every N ≥ 2?).
  Both are open variants, not the solved main statements. For Erdős 49, formal-conjectures
  PR #6292 reports f(N) = π(N) checked for every N up to 10^8, so a small counterexample
  search there is already done.
- Caveat: the homepage counters are animated. The figures above were read from the page text
  and may be off by the last digit.
